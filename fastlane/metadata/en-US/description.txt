We build the machines. This runs them.

Decaid is the official app from Decent Espresso for the DE1 family and Bengle. Same company, same obsession with the shot, now on iPhone and iPad.

Connect over Bluetooth or USB and Decaid takes care of the rest: it finds your machine and scale, reconnects when you come back, and puts the controls where you expect them.

KNOW YOUR SHOT
Pressure, flow, weight, group and mix temperature, shot timer, water level - live, while you brew. Start and stop shots, steam, flush or dispense hot water, and set brew and steam targets without leaving the screen.

DIAL IN YOUR COFFEE
Import and manage DE1 v2 profiles, keep duplicates out of your list, and make per-shot overrides when the coffee asks for it. Save the beans and grinders behind your best shots, with favourites one tap away.

BREW BY WEIGHT
Decent Scale, Half Decent Scale, or a supported Bluetooth scale from Acaia, Bookoo or Difluid. Run primary and auxiliary scales, auto-tare, and stop the shot at your target weight.

HISTORY YOU OWN
Every shot, with charts and per-shot detail. Export and restore your history, or bring it over from the previous Decent app so your archive stays in one place.

YOUR INTERFACE, YOUR CALL
The interface is community-built and yours to choose. Browse skins in-app, set your default, and keep them updated - Streamline.js is a good place to start. Skins are served locally by Decaid, so your workflow stays on your network.

ADD WHAT YOU NEED
Install community plugins for shot uploads, visualisation, readiness timers and profile tools. Every install asks for permission before it touches anything.

ONE DISPLAY OR TWO
Use an iPhone or iPad as the display. Connect from other devices too.

TUNE THE DETAILS
Presence-based auto-sleep, display brightness, keep-awake behaviour, light and dark themes. Set the machine up to match the way your counter actually works.

OPEN BY DESIGN
A local-first REST API, WebSocket streams, built-in API documentation and gateway modes for delegating control. Build your own interface, script your routine, or hand control to another tool.

Free, open source under GPLv3, and made by Decent Espresso LLC for Decent Espresso machines.
